Rating a site

People rate a site from 1 to 5 whole stars. 0 means not rated yet, and it never shows as a score. Averages show to one decimal, with the last star filled part of the way. Everything on this page works: rate, hover, remove and undo, and the summary and the prompt follow along.

Star

One shape at every size. The yellow fill is clipped from the left, so a star can be part filled. The outline keeps empty stars visible on any background.
